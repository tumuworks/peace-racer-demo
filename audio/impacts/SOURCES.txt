Impact sounds for src/impacts.ts. Every recording below is CC0 (public domain):
no credit required, free for commercial use.

One-shots: cut at the hit and faded out fast. High-passed at 45-150 Hz to drop
rumble and the recordings' own engines (the game has its own). Levelled so the
loudest 50 ms of each is about -12 dBFS RMS (the clattery ones a little under,
held back by the -1 dBFS peak limit). Mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit.
