All taken from the pack's "PNG (Transparent)" folder (512x512 each). The shape
is the source's alpha, unchanged except for scaling; the colour is set to pure
white everywhere so the game can tint it (the source darkens the colour towards
black where alpha is low, which would leave a grey fringe under normal
blending). Alpha under 6 cleared (stray near-invisible specks), then trimmed to
alpha 8 and above with 3 px of padding. Lanczos downscale. Saved as 8-bit
greyscale + alpha PNG.

Flames (256x128). Kenney's muzzle flashes stand upright (tip at the top), so
each is rotated 90 degrees clockwise: the flame points +x, its base is at the
left edge and its axis on the middle row. All four share one scale (0.454), so
the base lines up and only the length and outline change from frame to frame;
the white core starts about 25-29 px in from the left edge.
